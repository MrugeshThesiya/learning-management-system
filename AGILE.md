# Agile Methodology

## Project: Learning Management System (LMS)

### Team
- Mrugesh Thesiya (Lead / Full-stack)

### Vision
Deliver an LMS for instructors to publish courses and students to enroll, learn, and track progress.

### Sprints
- Sprint 1 (Week 1-2): Project setup, auth, base models
- Sprint 2 (Week 3-4): Course & lesson management
- Sprint 3 (Week 5-6): Enrollment & progress tracking
- Sprint 4 (Week 7-8): Quizzes & assignments
- Sprint 5 (Week 9-10): Search, categories, dashboards
- Sprint 6 (Week 11-12): Dockerization, CI/CD, polish

### Ceremonies
- Daily standup, sprint planning, review, retrospective
- Branch per feature, PR to main, conventional commits
